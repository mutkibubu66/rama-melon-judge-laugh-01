## The line

> Our meeting about having too many meetings ran over, so we booked a follow-up to discuss the action items from the meeting about meetings.

One original one-line joke about office life: the meeting called to fix meeting overload becomes the very thing it was called to fix — it runs over, spawns a follow-up, and the follow-up exists only to discuss the action items of the meeting about meetings. True to every office, with the turn that the cure and the disease are the same meeting.
